  heading: Quick Start Recommendation
  body: New to Meshtastic? We recommend starting with a Heltec V3 (~$20) or LilyGo T-Beam Supreme (~$50). Both are affordable, well supported by the current stable firmware, and available on Amazon with fast shipping. If you want a pocketable device with great battery life, consider the nRF52-based LilyGo T-Echo. Budget around $30-75 including an antenna upgrade.
